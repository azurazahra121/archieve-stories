She tapped her fingers on the paper bag. Her eyes were glued to the clock. It was 8:25 AM, only five minutes left before a customer would buy a croissant. Then a man walked into the bakery. It was him. She handed him the paper bag immediately.

His eyes widened for a moment, and he chuckled, "Thank you."

She nodded. "Have a great day!"

"You, too." He glanced at her as he scanned the QR code.

The man was about to walk out when she called him, "Thank you."

He smiled and waved his hand. The door shut behind him. He would never look back, but something about her voice felt alive and fresh to hear. He sighed. His steps remained steady towards his office.

He arrived ten minutes before it started. The coffee sat nicely beside the paper bag on his desk. Everything looked structured as well as his business suit. The ocean blue shirt looked so neat.
